Good to know

Do I need any experience?

None at all. Edirik has taught first-timers since 2008, and most people are up and riding within their first session. If you can swim and follow a few cues, you can wakeboard.

When are you open?

Saturdays and Sundays, roughly 9am to 6pm. Message us to lock in a slot — weekends fill up in high season.

Where do we meet?

We're at Bucutiweg in Oranjestad, Aruba. On Google Maps, search for "Pure Diving Aruba" — that's our spot. We'll send a pin and parking info once your time is confirmed.

What should I bring?

Swimwear, reef-safe sunscreen, a towel, and water. We provide boards, vests, and everything else. Leave phones and valuables on shore or in our dry bag.

What if the weather is bad?

Aruba is dry and windy but rarely un-rideable. If conditions aren't safe we reschedule or refund in full — no questions asked.

Is there an age or weight limit?

Riders from about age 8 and up. No upper limit — we instruct plenty of adults trying it for the first time. Contact us if you're unsure.
